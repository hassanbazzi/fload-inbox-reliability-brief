Replacement boundary

One runtime. Existing domain owners.

Engine owns

  • Durable progress and wake delivery
  • Timers, safe retries and restart recovery
  • Operational queues, concurrency and visibility
⇄Calls

Fload owns

  • Exact approval, revision, actor and permanent receipt
  • Fresh access, source and native-target checks
  • Guarded provider calls, uncertainty and readback
Acceptance → execution: one atomic enqueue, or a durable SQL obligation with idempotent delivery. Never a one-shot trigger after commit.

The experiment must remove replaced scheduling and recovery machinery; claims used for physical safety remain until an equivalent contract is proven. Keeping two runtimes in charge of the same obligation is not consolidation.

The non-negotiable failure case

Apple accepted. The worker disappeared.

  1. Retain intentCommit the exact approved attempt before sending.
  2. Apple appliesThe native POST/PATCH succeeds externally.
  3. Crash before captureNo trusted response or engine checkpoint remains.
  4. Recover by evidenceFind the unfinished attempt. Read back; do not blindly resend.

An engine can replay an unrecorded activity. “Exactly once” orchestration does not make Apple part of its transaction. Fresh authorization is checked at the physical boundary; a cached permission result is history.

What earns a decision

One comparable journey. Real failure injection.

Resolve before adoption

  1. Storage policy: engine journals contain generic serialized payloads. DBOS also has JSONB attributes. The selected implementation uses the stock vendor schema; Fload domain schemas remain strict. IDs-only inputs do not remove vendor serialization.DBOS schema evidence
  2. Delivery: prove atomic enqueue or durable SQL handoff, including rollback and lost acknowledgement.
  3. Operations: prove recovery across replicas, weeks-long waits, deployments and backup restoration.

Run on every finalist

  1. Kill after acceptance; recover one obligation.
  2. Wait weeks, deploy, duplicate/late wake: preserve approval and target.
  3. Apple accepts, then crash: readback before resend.
  4. Revoke access or retire credentials during the wait: no unauthorized send.
  5. Cut over old/new workers: one permitted writer for the current ASC app resource, even across tenants.

Measure custom code removed, recovery latency, connections, storage and operational burden.
